# Wire database and test campaign

Generated by `python -m cable_thermal wire-study`. Model: the paper's radial FDM/TDMA model (9 nodes per material, still air, emissivity 0.85, copper α = 0.00393 1/K) with Churchill–Chu natural convection (Eq. 25 results in brackets where shown). Ambient 30 °C unless stated. Data sources and modelling choices: docs/WIRE_DATABASE.md.
